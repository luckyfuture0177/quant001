常用策略模板

双均线金叉/死叉
buy: MA5 > MA10 and MA5 > MA20
sell: MA5 < MA10
MACD 金叉
buy: DIF > DEA and MACD > 0
sell: DIF < DEA
RSI 超买超卖
buy: RSI6 < 30
sell: RSI6 > 70
布林带突破
buy: close > UP
sell: close < DN
量价配合
buy: close > MA5 and vol > VolMA5 * 1.5
sell: close < MA5 or vol < VolMA10
KDJ 金叉
buy: K > D and J < 20
sell: K < D and J > 80
止盈止损（以买入价为基准）
buy: close > MA5 and close > MA20
sell: close < buy * 0.9 or close > buy * 1.1
多指标组合
buy: MA5 > MA10 and close > MA20 and MACD > 0 and RSI6 < 70
sell: close < MA5 or MACD < 0 or RSI6 > 80
